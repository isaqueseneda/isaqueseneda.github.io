The Pringles can grew. And so can you.

To celebrate the permanently bigger Pringles can, we created Pringheels — a limited-edition wearable fashion piece with integrated wheels that instantly makes you taller.

Art Direction
Concept Creation
Product Design
INSIGHT
Pringles made the can bigger — but nobody noticed. A size change needs a cultural moment, not a label update.
STRATEGY
Turn the size upgrade into something you can feel. Literally. If the can grew, so should you. Make height the metaphor.
IDEA
Pringheels — roller shoes shaped like Pringles cans. A limited-edition wearable that turns the bigger can into a fashion piece. You snack taller.
TONE
Absurd but dead serious. Like a real product launch for something nobody asked for — but everyone wants once they see it.
