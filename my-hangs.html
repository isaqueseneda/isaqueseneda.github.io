How it started

A weekend in the
south of Germany.

I spent hours hunting down everyone who lived near one city and pulled them together for a weekend.

One total newbie wandered in, had the time of their life, and left asking: “when's the next camp?”

🏰
The insight

You don't recruit people to a community.
You let them taste it.

So let's be generous and selfish at once — help new people find the SIG, purely by travelling with our friends.

The part that's a bit magic

Point your AI agent at it.

MY-Hangs ships with an API and an MCP server. So you can just ask your assistant:

“Who from the SIG is around Heidelberg the weekend of the 8th, and does anyone have a couch?”
