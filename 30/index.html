What to pack and know

  1. Sauna: bring a swimsuit, a towel, and flip-flops if you have them.
  2. Food on Saturday: there's no seated dinner. We grab takeout beforehand, bring snacks and drinks, and buy drinks at the bar. The gym sells food too, but it's pricey.
  3. Gear: comfy shoes and clothes you can move or lounge in, a small backpack, and a power bank.
  4. Lockers: the gym is a public venue, but you can lock your things in a locker and relax like at camp.
