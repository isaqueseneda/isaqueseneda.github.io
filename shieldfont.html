INSIGHT
Every sentence you publish can be harvested to train AI — no consent, no credit, no opt-out. Writers became the raw material, and nobody asked them.
STRATEGY
You can't stop the scrapers, so poison the well. Split what a human reads from what a machine reads, and let the machine choke on the difference.
IDEA
ShieldFont — a typeface with a cipher baked in. OpenType ligatures remap the glyphs so readers see your real words, while the source code AI scrapes reads as nonsense.
CRAFT
A real, installable font plus a one-line CSS drop-in and a live encoder. Open-source, free, and already protecting writing at shieldfont.org.
