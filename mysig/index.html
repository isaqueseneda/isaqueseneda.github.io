The Concept

A RECURSIVE ACRONYM (LIKE WINE)

MY = My Youth, Infinitely.

Like WINE is Not an Emulator or GNU's Not Unix: a self-referential acronym where the definition loops back on itself. A fractal loop that belongs to whoever steps into it.

THE FRACTAL PRINCIPLE

Self-Similar At Every Scale.

Two friends playing chess at 3am, a 40-person regional weekend, or 3,000+ members gathered across Europe: the same culture, replicating naturally everywhere.

The Pronoun Revolution
