You're not imagining it.

Owners have reported noise with noise cancelling on since the first AirPods Pro, in 2019. Here's what's known, and what you can do.

  • Every generation since 2019. The first AirPods Pro mostly crackled; the third mostly hisses. Counted on 30 October 2026: 76 threads, more than 3,200 comments, and 23,549 “Me too” clicks on Apple's forum (clicks, not people).
  • Apple admitted it once. In 2020 Apple offered free repairs for first-generation AirPods Pro with “crackling or static sounds”. That program has ended. About AirPods Pro 2 and 3, Apple has said nothing publicly.
  • Worst in quiet rooms. It's loudest with noise cancelling on and nothing playing. Some owners hear it in Transparency or Adaptive mode instead.
  • Swaps don't always fix it. On every generation, some owners say the replacement pair made the same noise. One went through three pairs.
  • Not fixed for everyone. After a December 2025 update, some AirPods Pro 3 owners say the hiss went away. Others still hear it. Switching noise cancelling off stops it, but then they're ordinary earbuds.
  • Ear or sinus trouble? A blocked or stuck-open Eustachian tube can cause popping, clicking or hearing your own breathing, and earbuds that seal your ear make body sounds louder. Nobody has tested whether that makes the hiss worse. If it's you, add it to your story.

If your pair does it

  1. Name what you hear.

    Use the sounds above. Note which AirPods, which ear, since when, and your firmware (Settings, then your AirPods).

  2. Keep receipts, use your rights.

    In the EU, sellers owe a 2-year legal guarantee. If a repair or swap doesn't fix it, you can ask for a refund (in France: Code de la consommation, article L217-14).
